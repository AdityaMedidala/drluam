# CQR out-of-period confirmation on 2024

Analysis only. The app does not change, whatever the result.

**Status: rule committed on 25 Oct 2026, before any 2023–2024 data was fetched.** The rule section is not edited after this commit. Results are added below the line.

**Why 2024.** The CQR study (`docs/cqr.md`) was "promising, unconfirmed" on months that had all been seen before. No 2023 or 2024 data has been fetched or used by this project. So 2024 is an unseen period that can confirm or reject the recipe now, without waiting for Nov 2026+ data. It is out-of-period, not out-of-time: the wind fleet and the WINDFOR model in 2024 differ from 2026, which cuts both ways.

## Pre-registered rule

### Recipe: frozen exactly as in `docs/cqr.md`

Code: `analysis/ml_challenger.py` and `analysis/cqr.py` as of this commit. A small driver (`analysis/cqr_2024.py`) calls them with different dates and data folders only.

- **Model:** the same features, LightGBM parameters (`lightgbm==4.6.0`) and quantiles, sorted and floored at 0.
- **Retraining:** monthly, by **decision month** (D−1). The model for decision month M is trained on every hour delivered from the training start up to the day before M begins. No hour is scored by a model that saw it.
- **Calibration:** 28-day rolling CQR on out-of-sample predictions, using only hours whose outturn was known at the decision time (startTime + 30 min ≤ 09:20 UK on D−1).
  - Two-sided adjustment for the 80% range.
  - One-sided adjustment for P50–P95.
  - Upper α = 0.90 term for the pinball loss.
  - Fewer than 168 calibration hours means no adjustment for that day, and such days are counted.
- **Decision time:** 09:20 UK on D−1.
- **Current method:** the app's, unchanged.

### Data and periods

- **Training data:** delivery days from **1 Jan 2023** (the expanding window's start), always only data before each model's decision month.
- **Scored period:** delivery days **1 Jan – 31 Dec 2024**. Results are reported by **delivery month** (12 months).
  - Each scored day uses the model of its decision month, so 1 Jan 2024 is scored by the December 2023 model.
  - Each scored day is adjusted from its own 28-day calibration window.
  - Out-of-sample predictions are generated from the December 2023 decision month, which covers every calibration window.
- **Scored hours:** those with an outturn where the current method has a full week of residuals (168), as in the earlier studies. All methods are scored on the same hours.
- **Fetch:** `backend/scripts/fetch_data.py --first-delivery 2023-01-01 --last-delivery 2024-12-31 --out-dir analysis/data-2023-2024`. The CSVs are gitignored; the manifest with sha256 is committed.

### Data sufficiency: stop rule, applied before any model is trained

Before running, report:
- outturn and forecast availability per year;
- the WINDFOR publish schedule per year (runs per day, publish times).

**Stop** without running, and report, if either holds:
- 2023 outturn is missing for more than 10% of half-hours;
- the decision forecast (latest run at or before 09:20 UK on D−1) is missing for more than 10% of 2023 or 2024 target hours.

If stopped, the recipe is not changed to work around it.

### Metrics

As in `docs/cqr.md`, for the current method, LightGBM raw and LightGBM + CQR:
- 80% range coverage and mean width;
- pinball loss (mean over α = 0.10, 0.50, 0.90);
- MAE (of q₀.₅₀);
- P50–P95 hit rates against target;
- the cost backtest at £100 short / £30 long per MWh.

These are reported per delivery month and pooled.

**Uncertainty:** day-block bootstrap (10,000 resamples of delivery days, seed 0, 95% percentile intervals), pooled, for:
- CQR − current, pinball loss;
- CQR − current, MAE;
- CQR coverage.

### Verdict wording, fixed in advance

**"confirmed out-of-period"** only if all three hold on 2024:
1. CQR's 80% range coverage is within 75–85% (inclusive) in **at least 9 of the 12** delivery months;
2. CQR's **pooled** 2024 coverage is within 75–85%;
3. CQR's pooled pinball loss is lower than the current method's, **and** the day-block bootstrap 95% interval of CQR − current lies entirely **below zero**.

**"not confirmed"** otherwise. The current method's 2024 results are reported alongside whatever happens.

Even if confirmed, the app does not change on this evidence alone. A method change would follow the project's rule (pre-registered, confirmed on Nov 2026+ data).

### Leakage guards

- The existing tests run unchanged (`analysis/tests/`). The script's runtime assertions apply to every calibration window:
  - every outturn is known at the decision time;
  - every prediction comes from a model trained before its own decision month.
- The feature-timing check (each row's latest information is at or before its decision time) is asserted on the 2023–2024 feature rows.

### Run

Exactly once, after the data report. The output is saved to `analysis/cqr-2024-result.txt`.
